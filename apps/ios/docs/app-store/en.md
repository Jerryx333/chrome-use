## Description
chrome-use Remote is the phone remote for the chrome-use agent panel on your computer. Start a conversation, follow the agent as it works in your real, signed-in Chrome, and answer its approval requests wherever you are.

Everything runs on your own computer. The phone connects to it directly over your Tailscale network, your own Cloudflare tunnel or your local network; there is no chrome-use server in between. Every message is end-to-end encrypted with keys that only your phone and your computer hold.

- Pair in seconds: run `chrome-use mobile pair` and scan the QR code.
- Conversations: start new ones with Claude Code, Codex and the other agents you use, send text and up to five images, watch progress live, stop a turn.
- Approvals: see exactly what the agent wants to do and allow or decline it; questions from the agent are answered right on the phone.
- Notifications: get told when the agent needs you or finishes. Notification contents are encrypted on your computer; our push service only relays ciphertext.
- Screenshots: see the agent's browser tab on demand.
- Schedules: run a scheduled task now, or turn it on or off.
- Several computers, an optional Face ID lock, and nine languages.

Your phone never gets more power than your computer allows: conversations started from the phone cannot run commands, and full access stays on the desktop unless you turn it on there.

Requires chrome-use on a Mac, Windows or Linux computer. Free to try for 14 days, then a one-time purchase.
